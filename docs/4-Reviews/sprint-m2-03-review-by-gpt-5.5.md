## Summary

The M2-03 documents are directionally aligned with the product and architecture boundaries: CMS scope is limited to news/cases, M2-02 auth/sanitizer/media boundaries are reused, and public content status uses `404/410`. The documents still need sharper implementation contracts before coding, especially for public snapshot semantics, transition side effects, SEO schema, ESA purge construction, audit transactions, and evidence labeling. These are document gaps, not production failures, but they should be resolved in review-confirm before M2-03 implementation starts.
