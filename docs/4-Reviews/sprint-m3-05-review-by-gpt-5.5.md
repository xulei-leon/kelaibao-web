## Summary

The FR and Sprint are directionally aligned with the source-of-truth: they preserve one site/app/CMS, reject direct SQL and schema changes, require claims evidence or weakened copy, keep discovery files on new public routes, and maintain Production `BLOCKED` when real evidence is missing.

The main gaps are contract precision: CMS inventory source modes, evidence file schemas, claims freshness/placeholder rules, release validator status fields, inherited 16-blocker continuity, scan allowlists, and final Done/commit gates should be tightened before implementation.
